# NNN. Title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decided by:** who made the call (for example the product owners, the Meta team, or the team)
- **Issue:** #NN

One short file per significant decision, named `NNN-short-name.md` (for example `001-scoped-token.md`). Copy this template, keep the four headings, and write each part in a few sentences. Once a decision is accepted, do not edit it to change the decision: write a new record that supersedes it, and update the old one's status line.

## Context

What problem or question forced a decision, and the facts that constrain it. Link to the issue, the contract, the design spec or the handbook section.

## Decision

What we decided, in one or two sentences, stated plainly.

## Alternatives considered

Each serious alternative, and why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we must now do or never do because of this decision.
